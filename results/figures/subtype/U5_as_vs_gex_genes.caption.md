**Figure U5. The splicing and expression models of a cohort read different genes.** For each cohort (patients in
brackets) and splicing view: how many genes are in both the top 50 genes of the splicing model (a gene ranked by its
best event) and the top 50 of the cohort's GEX model, counted within the genes both views could select in that
cohort (D-41). Under independent draws the expected count is 0.2–1.6 (median 0.5); the CSV holds it with the fold
enrichment, the hypergeometric raw p and BH q (family named in the CSV).

The median overlap is 1 gene of 50 and 55 of 176 pairs share none; 7 pairs exceed chance at q < 0.05. The largest
overlaps are ESCA RI: 8 (ATP7B, ERN2, HNF1A, IKBKB-DT, IL17RB, KIF12, LGALS4, MYO7B); HNSC_HPV RI: 6 (CBARP, KLHL35,
MIR9-3HG, MSL3, STAG3, TMEM161B-DT); SARC MXE: 6 (ACTN1, LDB3, PDLIM7, SNAP25, TPM1, TPM2).
