**Do the exons that mark MSI in one cancer mark it in the other two?**

Every splicing event in the top 5 of the MSI class of the UCEC, STAD or COAD model (any view) whose AUROC, MSI
against the rest of the cohort, is at least 0.25 from 0.5 in the same direction in all three cohorts: 10 events
(AUROC in each cell; red = higher in MSI). • = in that cohort's MSI top 5; a cell without • is a replication in a
cohort whose model did not pick the event. No model is involved in the AUROC; the patients with a value in each cell
(MSI and rest) are in the CSV. Right: the D-27 audit, as in U6. Exploratory (A-4): the step was added after the same
CCNDBP1 exon was seen in the MSI top 5 of all three models; it then tests every event of every shared class.

7 of the 10 events were chosen independently by at least two of the three models. CCNDBP1's 57-bp exon is included
at a median UCEC 0.20 vs 0.06; STAD 0.28 vs 0.04; COAD 0.28 vs 0.04 (MSI vs rest). All 10 replication cells have
Mann-Whitney raw p < 7.3e-05; BH q over every cross-cohort test of every shared class is in the CSV.
