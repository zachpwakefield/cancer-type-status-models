**Figure RPM. The data do not separate branch-point geometry from hnRNP C.** Held-out cancers, junction reads;
outside the claim's verdict (family RPM). Change in inclusion = an exon's mean adjusted PSI in the MMRd tumours
minus the MMRp mean of the same cancer, from junction reads only (rMATS JC); a tumour's adjusted PSI is its
deviation from the cancer's median minus its mean deviation over background exons of the same baseline band. Mean
change with a descriptive 95% interval; dashed = the smallest effect of interest. (a) Rising arm = acceptor T-run ≥
20 nt with the sequence-predicted branch point ≥ 36 nt from the AG; T-run upstream of the branch point = exons
without an acceptor T-run of ≥ 10 nt whose intron holds a T-run of ≥ 20 nt upstream of the branch point (shield
exons). Shortening that run cannot bring the branch point closer to the AG, so the geometry reading predicts no rise
there and a larger rise in the rising arm. (b) Eligible runs of ≥ 20 nt (rising arm, or with a near or no branch
point) inside an antisense Alu and outside one. hnRNP C binds long U-runs and represses Alu-derived exons, so the
hnRNP C reading predicts that shield exons rise too and that runs inside an antisense Alu rise more. Numbers = exons
measured. The tests compare classes within strata of baseline band × coverage tertile (and run-length bins for (b)),
keeping the exons that have a partner there (92 against 64 exons, 45 effective controls for (a); 131 against 42
exons, 19 effective controls for (b)), so they differ from the raw difference of the dots.
