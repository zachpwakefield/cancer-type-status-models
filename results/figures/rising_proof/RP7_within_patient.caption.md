**Figure RP7. MMR-deficient tumours rise above their own normal tissue, mostly in training cancers.** Tumour–normal
pairs (the tumour and a Solid Tissue Normal file of the same patient) of the cancers with at least one MMR-deficient
pair: BRCA, COAD, PRAD, STAD, UCEC. MMRd = MMR-deficient (≥ 10 MC3 homopolymer deletions); MMRp = MMR-proficient.
Each line joins a patient's own normal tissue to the tumour: the rising-arm level (rising arm = acceptor T-run ≥ 20
nt with the sequence-predicted branch point ≥ 36 nt from the AG) of each sample, its mean PSI deviation from the
cancer's median over the rising-arm exons measured in both samples, minus its mean deviation over background exons
of the same baseline band. The tumour minus the normal is the pair arm that the test uses. (a) The 12 MMR-deficient
patients; 10 are in training cancers (COAD 6, STAD 3, UCEC 1), so the test is not held out (family RPP). (b) The 200
MMR-proficient patients of the same cancers. Orange = own normal tissue, blue = tumour; black line = mean.

- RP7, mean over MMRd pairs of the pair arm minus the MMRp mean of the same cancer; MMR labels permuted among each
  cancer’s pairs (10,000): +0.0194, p = 1.0e-4, supported. RPC7, the falling arm: −0.0045, p = 0.007, supported.
- Of the two held-out MMR-deficient pairs, the BRCA pair lies well above its cancer's MMR-proficient mean, while the
  PRAD pair is about level with it (single-pair values are kept locally; committed cells under 5 pairs are blanked,
  A-24).
