**Figure RP6. The rise marks mismatch repair rather than mutation load, but four other subtype contrasts (three in
sarcoma) move it more than MSI.** Held-out cancers, junction reads. MMRd = MMR-deficient (≥ 10 MC3 homopolymer
deletions); MMRp = MMR-proficient; hypermutated MMRp = ≥ 500 single-nucleotide variants without MMR deficiency. (a)
Each tumour's rising-arm level (mean adjusted PSI over its rising-arm exons; rising arm = acceptor T-run ≥ 20 nt
with the sequence-predicted branch point ≥ 36 nt from the AG) as a z within its cancer against the cancer's
non-hypermutated MMRp tumours; boxes = quartiles, line = median, whiskers = 5th–95th percentiles, dots = tumours
(not drawn for the other MMRp tumours). (b) The shift of the rising-arm exons between two classes (D-70's RV1 in
junction reads: 20 tumours drawn from each class, mean over rising-arm exons of the difference in mean adjusted PSI,
mean over 50 draws), for MSI (20 held-out MMRd tumours, each with a random MMRp tumour of its cancer) and for each
of the 57 non-MSI subtype contrasts of the subtype task (two classes of one cohort with ≥ 20 tumours each, MSI, POLE
and MMRd tumours removed), ranked; blue = MSI, grey = the 57 other contrasts, dark grey and labelled = those above
MSI. The null contrasts are not matched by cancer, while the MSI draws pair tumours within cancer.
