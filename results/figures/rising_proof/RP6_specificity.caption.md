**Figure RP6. The arm separates mismatch repair from mutation load, but leiomyosarcoma contrasts move it as much.**
Held-out cancers, junction reads. (a) Each tumour's R_up arm (mean adjusted PSI over its R_up exons) as a z within
its cancer against the cancer's non-hypermutated MMRp tumours: MMRd (50), hypermutated MMRp (408; hypermutated
without MMR deficiency) and the other MMRp tumours (7,227); boxes = quartiles, whiskers = 5th–95th percentiles, dots
= tumours (other MMRp not drawn). (b) The MSI statistic s (D-70's RV1 in junction reads: 20 held-out MMRd tumours,
each with a random MMRp tumour of its cancer; mean over R_up exons of the difference in mean adjusted PSI; mean over
50 draws) against the same statistic for each of the 57 non-MSI subtype contrasts of the subtype task (two classes
of one cohort with ≥ 20 tumours each, MSI, POLE and MMRd tumours removed; 20 tumours drawn from each class, 50
draws); contrasts at or above MSI's |s| are labelled. The null contrasts are not matched by cancer, while the MSI
draws pair tumours within cancer.
