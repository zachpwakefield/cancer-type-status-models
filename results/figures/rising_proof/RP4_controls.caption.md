**Figure RP4. The rise exceeds that of three other long tracts; the poly-A and donor-side contrasts were
underpowered.** Held-out cancers, junction reads. Rising arm = acceptor T-run ≥ 20 nt with the sequence-predicted
branch point ≥ 36 nt from the AG. Each control has no acceptor T-run longer than 7 nt and shares one other feature
with the rising arm: a long pyrimidine tract that is not a microsatellite (with a distant branch point), an acceptor
inside an Alu, a poly-A run (≥ 15 nt) at the acceptor, a T-run of ≥ 20 nt deep in the intron, or one after the
donor. (a) The pre-registered contrast: the rising arm's change minus the control's, within strata of baseline band
× coverage tertile, coarsened until each rising-arm stratum holds ≥ 3 controls; filled = supported, hollow = not
resolved. (b) The difference each contrast could detect with 80% power at two-sided 0.05 (2.80 SD of its
exon-permutation null, rebuilt with step 62's code and seed; normal approximation; descriptive), against the whole
rise of the rising arm (blue line). Light grey bars = the three contrasts that were supported, dark grey = the two
that were not resolved (poly-A and donor-side runs); the shade marks the verdict, not the bar's length.

- RP4b, rising arm minus long pyrimidine tract: +0.0089, p = 5.0e-4, supported. RP4e, rising arm minus Alu acceptor:
  +0.0066, p = 5.0e-4, supported. RP4c, rising arm minus T-run deep in the intron: +0.0081, p = 0.008, supported.
- RP4a, rising arm minus poly-A run: +0.0042, p = 0.25, not resolved. RP4d, rising arm minus T-run after the donor:
  +0.0047, p = 0.088, not resolved. p = the larger of the exon-permutation p (0.25, 0.088) and the
  MMR-label-permutation p (0.005, 0.010).
- These two are underpowered rather than negative: detectable differences about 0.010 for poly-A (61 controls, 27
  effective) and 0.008 for donor-side runs; if the controls did not move at all, the power would be about 0.48 and
  0.72. The controls' own means: poly-A +0.0016 (p = 0.10), donor-side +0.0019 (p = 0.15).
- 61 of the 92 rising-arm acceptors lie in an Alu. For the other 31, rising arm minus distant branch point without a
  run: +0.0057, p = 0.068.
