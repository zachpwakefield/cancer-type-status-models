**Figure RP4. The rise exceeds long pyrimidine tracts, deep T-runs and Alu acceptors; poly-A and donor-side runs are
not resolved.** Held-out cancers, junction reads. Mean delta (dot) of the rising arm and of each control class, with
a descriptive 95% interval (RP2a's construction); dashed = SESOI. Controls have no acceptor T-run longer than 7 nt
and differ from R_up in one feature: a distant branch point and no run (B sample), a long pyrimidine tract that is
not a microsatellite with a distant branch point, an acceptor inside an Alu, a poly-A run (≥ 15 nt) at the acceptor,
a T-run of ≥ 20 nt deep in the intron, or after the donor. The tests compare R_up with each control within strata of
baseline band × coverage tertile, coarsened until each R_up stratum holds ≥ 3 controls (stratum-weighted difference
of mean delta; right-hand columns), so they differ from the raw difference of the dots. p = the larger of the
exon-label (class) permutation p and the MMR-label permutation p.

- RP3a, R_up minus distant branch point without a run: +0.0074, p = 5.0e-4, supported.
- RP4b, R_up minus long pyrimidine tract: +0.0089, p = 5.0e-4, supported. RP4e, R_up minus Alu acceptor: +0.0066, p
  = 5.0e-4, supported.
- RP4c, R_up minus deep-intron T-run: +0.0081, p = 0.008, supported. RP4a, R_up minus poly-A: +0.0042, p = 0.25, not
  resolved. RP4d, R_up minus donor-side T-run: +0.0047, p = 0.088, not resolved.
- RP4a and RP4d are underpowered rather than negative. Their exon-permutation p (0.25, 0.088) is far above their
  label-permutation p (0.005, 0.010). The SD of the exon-permutation null is 0.0037 for poly-A (61 controls, 27
  effective) and 0.0028 for donor-side runs, so the difference detectable with 80% power is about 0.010 and 0.008
  (normal approximation), larger than RP2a itself; if the controls did not move at all, the power would be about
  0.48 and 0.72. Own means: poly-A +0.0016 (p = 0.10), donor-side +0.0019 (p = 0.15).
- 61 of the 92 R_up acceptors lie in an Alu. For the other 31, R_up minus distant branch point: +0.0057, p = 0.068.
