**Figure RP2. The rising arm rises in held-out junction reads, and a few of its exons carry most of the rise.**
Held-out cancers. MMRd = MMR-deficient (≥ 10 MC3 homopolymer deletions); MMRp = MMR-proficient; change in inclusion
= an exon's mean adjusted PSI in the MMRd tumours minus the MMRp mean of the same cancer, from junction reads only
(rMATS JC); a tumour's adjusted PSI is its deviation from the cancer's median minus its mean deviation over
background exons of the same baseline band. Exons need ≥ 5 MMRd tumours measured. (a) Mean change of each class with
its 95% interval (studentized SE over MMR labels permuted within cancer, Welch–Satterthwaite df; the rising- and
falling-arm intervals are those of the tests, the background's is descriptive). Rising arm = acceptor T-run ≥ 20 nt
with the sequence-predicted branch point ≥ 36 nt from the AG; falling arm = acceptor T-run of 10–19 nt; background =
exons without an acceptor T-run longer than 5 nt (the B sample). (b) The 92 rising-arm exons ranked by their change;
dark = the 10 largest, which hold 81% of the summed change; line = background exons' changes at the same quantiles
(rank k of 92 against the background's (1 − (k − 0.5) / 92) quantile; 4,756 exons); the three largest labelled with
their gene.

- RP2a, rising-arm mean change: +0.0070 (95% interval 0.0033 to 0.0107), p = 1.0e-3, supported; the whole interval
  lies above the smallest effect of interest. RPC2a, falling arm: −0.0030 (−0.0047 to −0.0013), p = 0.002,
  supported.
- RP2c, share of rising-arm exons that rise (studentized change > c = 3.02) minus that of 5 matched background exons
  each: +0.076, p = 7.0e-4, supported; RP2b, per tumour (rising-arm level, MMRd − MMRp): +0.0084, p = 5.0e-4,
  supported.
- The class mean is carried by a minority of exons: median +0.0019 (sensitivity p = 5.0e-4); 62% of rising-arm exons
  above 0; the 10 largest hold 81% of the summed change, and the mean of the other 82 is +0.0015, below the smallest
  effect of interest. Gene level (change averaged within gene first): +0.0068 (0.0032 to 0.0105).
