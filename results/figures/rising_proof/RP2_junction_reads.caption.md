**Figure RP2. Junction reads confirm the rise in held-out cancers, carried by a minority of the exons.** Held-out
cancers, junction reads only (rMATS JC). Per exon, delta = the mean adjusted PSI of the MMRd tumours minus the MMRp
mean of the same cancer (≥ 5 MMRd tumours measured); a tumour's adjusted PSI is its deviation from the cancer's
median, minus its mean deviation over background exons of the same baseline band. (a) Mean delta of each class with
its 95% interval (studentized SE over MMR labels permuted within cancer, Welch–Satterthwaite df; the intervals of
R_up and R_down are those of RP2a and RPC2a, the background's is descriptive); hollow = R_up median; dashed = the
smallest effect of interest (SESOI, 0.003). Background = the B sample of exons without an acceptor T-run longer than
5 nt (4,756). (b) The 92 R_up exons ranked by delta; dark blue = the 10 largest; grey line = the background exons'
deltas at the same quantiles (rank k of 92 against the background's (1 − (k − 0.5) / 92) quantile; 4,756 exons;
middle 95% −0.0313 to 0.0302); lines = R_up mean, median and the SESOI; the three largest labelled.

- RP2a, R_up mean delta: +0.0070 (95% interval 0.0033 to 0.0107), p = 1.0e-3, supported; RPC2a, R_down: −0.0030
  (−0.0047 to −0.0013), p = 0.002, supported.
- RP2c, share of R_up exons rising (studentized delta > c = 3.02) minus that of 5 matched background exons each:
  +0.076, p = 7.0e-4, supported; RP2b, per tumour (R_up arm, MMRd − MMRp): +0.0084, p = 5.0e-4, supported.
- The class mean is carried by a minority of exons: median +0.0019 (median sensitivity p = 5.0e-4); 62% of R_up
  exons above 0; the 10 largest hold 81% of the summed delta, and the mean of the other 82 is +0.0015, below the
  SESOI. Gene level (delta averaged within gene first): +0.0068 (0.0032 to 0.0105).
