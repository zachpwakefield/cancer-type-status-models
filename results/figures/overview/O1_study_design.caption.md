**Figure O1. Nine views of the same RNA-seq samples, and the analyses each cancer enters.** (a) The nine views, each
modelled separately. Each splicing view compares two isoforms (top: the form whose use is measured, with the
alternative segment in blue; bottom: the other form). AFE and ALE, alternative first and last exons; HIT, the hybrid
internal-terminal index of an exon that can start or end a transcript; SE, a skipped (cassette) exon; A5SS and A3SS,
alternative 5′ and 3′ splice sites; MXE, mutually exclusive exons; RI, a retained intron; GEX, gene expression from
read counts. (b) Cases per TCGA project (9,034 tumour, 695 normal; log scale; dashed line = 10). Below: the 15
projects with at least 10 normal cases, which have a tumour-status model; the number of subtype cohorts drawn from
each project; and the tumours with at least 10 MC3 homopolymer deletions (MMR-deficient), in bold blue for the three
cancers that trained the MSI models. Every project enters the 34-class cancer-type model.
